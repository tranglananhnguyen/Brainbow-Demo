About Brainbow

The Brainbow Book Management System is designed to centralize and harmonize operations across Rainbow Books' head office, retail stores, and franchise network. Our mission is to bridge misalignment among stakeholders and provide a unified data backbone for sales, inventory, events, and publishing operations.

We enable strategic consistency, transparent KPI distribution, and equitable event hosting across all locations. With AI-powered insights and real-time analytics, Rainbow Books can make data-driven decisions that drive growth and enhance customer experience.
